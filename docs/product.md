# Investobot MVP

Investobot is a local portfolio ledger and research assistant. Humans supply
reviewed account observations, confirm accepted facts, approve trading policies,
and execute any trades themselves. The app never logs into a broker or trades.

The offline MVP supports exact holdings/cash/lot accounting, dated actual
performance, conservative tax-cost budgets, contribution-first proposals,
recursive fund exposure, hypothetical backtests, cited specialty research and
local text/vector retrieval. SQLite owns financial truth; agents orchestrate
documented commands rather than calculate financial results.

The dashboard is a read-only viewer of accepted facts and saved plans/backtests;
saved comparisons use persisted results only. All mutations and new calculations
belong to agent-run CLI commands with human review. Contributions-only never
sells; rebalance has no enforced calendar, preceding-trade or drift-threshold gate.

Unknown facts remain unknown and produce typed blockers or review requirements.
REAL and SYNTHETIC evidence cannot combine into actionable proposals. Current
quotes do not replace accepted marks or historical evidence. Research cannot
change holdings, classifications, prices or trading permissions.

Vanguard, Fidelity NetBenefits and PNC ingestion remains PROVISIONAL pending
reviewed anonymized original HTML. FMP remains UNVERIFIED pending human live
entitlement validation. Named-contribution rights, source truth and actual agent
compliance require independent human review. Synthetic acceptance establishes
development behavior only. Phase 2 real-page/live-provider review follows Phase 1
foundation acceptance. See [current status](implementation/foundation-stabilization-status.md)
for outstanding delivery work and [workflows](workflows.md) for supported intents.
