# Tax analysis boundaries

This software is analytical tooling, not tax preparation. It never places trades or adjusts imported broker basis. Broker-provided basis remains authoritative; missing basis or lot detail produces `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` rather than a guessed value.

Monetary calculations use Decimal. A lot's stored cost basis applies to its original quantity; remaining and proposed-sale basis are proportional to that original quantity. Long-term classification requires a sale later than the acquisition's one-year anniversary. Sale fees reduce proceeds and are allocated by sold shares, with any Decimal residue assigned to the final selected lot. Capital-loss deduction limits, carryforwards, bracket changes and filing-specific rules remain outside these estimates.

Supported selection methods are FIFO, LIFO, HIFO, explicit ordered SPECIFIC lot IDs and MIN_TAX. MIN_TAX requires ordinary-income and long-term capital-gains rates; it sorts available lots by estimated current tax effect per share. Optional NIIT and state rates add to each term's rate. Negative estimated tax effect represents the rate-based effect of a loss, not guaranteed usable tax savings. Absent rates yield gains/losses without invented tax-dollar estimates.

Taxable analysis requires resolved lots covering the entire position exactly, valid acquisition dates and known basis. Unresolved inventory suppresses gains in sale estimates, lot inspection and harvesting. An explicitly unknown source timestamp prevents a reliable estimate; import time is not a source valuation date. Legacy rows without this field remain readable with a freshness warning. A known source date before the analysis date is disclosed as `STALE`; no arbitrary freshness interval is invented. Verify current holdings before acting.

Sale output includes `realized_short_term_gain_loss`, `realized_long_term_gain_loss`, `estimated_current_tax_effect`, `potential_wash_sale_conflict` and `tax_data_completeness`; older field names remain available. Completeness distinguishes lot inventory, loaded household history and source freshness. Incomplete household history preserves independently known gains but warns that wash-sale treatment cannot be established. Tax-free and tax-deferred sales report zero taxable gains without claiming their eventual withdrawal treatment is tax-free.

Retirement sales do not require taxable lot inventory: completeness reports `NOT_REQUIRED`, while actual source freshness and household-history limitations remain visible. Missing acquisition dates in taxable lots return insufficient data and incomplete wash-sale history rather than an internal error or a no-conflict claim.

Wash-sale checks examine known same-security BUY activity and dated lots across all loaded household accounts, including Traditional and Roth IRAs, within the inclusive 30 days before and after a proposed loss sale. Explicit ISIN/CUSIP/FIGI bridges resolve different canonical IDs through the shared security identity mechanism. Results identify potential conflicts, insufficient history, or no known conflict. They do not determine whether different securities are legally substantially identical, infer complete histories from account snapshots, allocate disallowed losses, or alter basis. Future household activity and unknown outside accounts or spouse activity can change actual tax treatment. `history_complete` is an explicit assertion about loaded activity, not a guarantee that future transactions will not occur.

Harvesting candidates are taxable lots with known basis, known current price and an unrealized loss. Candidates are analytical facts, not trade recommendations. Valuations use ledger snapshots and disclose their dates; the tax module does not retrieve current market prices.

A partial sale of a recently acquired lot keeps the retained shares visible as a potential replacement acquisition. The reported conflict quantity for that lot is the remaining quantity after the proposed sale. Mixed sales check each loss lot even when total sale proceeds produce a net gain. Conflict records are conservative flags, not an allocation of legally disallowed loss.

Configured tax estimates are labeled `UNADJUSTED_POTENTIAL_EFFECT` and explicitly exclude wash-sale adjustments. They must not be described as guaranteed savings. Accounts with `OTHER` tax status return insufficient tax data rather than assuming a tax-free sale.
