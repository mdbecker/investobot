# Exposure and quantitative analysis
`portfolio exposure` combines direct securities with every available fund constituent. Decimal weights are fractions; output percentages are percentage points. Contributions identify synthetic accounts and funds. Partial holdings allocate only their known fraction; missing/unsupported exposure is reported explicitly. Weights are never rescaled, and inconsistent totals over 100% leave the fund unknown. Cash is included in the household denominator. Sectors/asset classes only appear when supplied by structured data. Provider provenance remains visible in the result.
