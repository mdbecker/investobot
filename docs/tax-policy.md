# Annual tax allowance and loss-sale review

Investobot applies a conservative household USD planning allowance, rather than
predicting a final tax bill. Approve a tax-year policy with an annual dollar limit,
explicit short- and long-term marginal rates, sourced timestamped coverage of all
taxable accounts and earlier sales, and REAL or SYNTHETIC provenance. Missing
rates, source coverage or currencies remain unknown.

Only confirmed taxable sales consume the allowance. Each accepted executed lot
allocation preserves basis removed, allocated fee, holding term and realized gain.
The budget reads those immutable facts; later broker adjustments or remaining lots
cannot rewrite an earlier sale. Positive gains consume their applicable term rate.
Losses are reported separately and never replenish the allowance. Saved proposals,
retirement sales and hypothetical trades do not spend it. Headroom cannot fall
below zero. A proposed incremental positive tax cost exceeding headroom requires
review and blocks that sale.

Missing historical sale facts block an authoritative budget. Investobot does not
reconstruct them from current lots or quietly use zero. Supply reviewed original
execution evidence through an explicit confirmed correction workflow. The tax
budget's cash-only allowance flag means no tax-budget objection; ordinary account,
cash, identity, price and permission checks still apply to purchases.

Household loss-sale review inspects known acquisitions in every configured account,
including IRAs, over the inclusive thirty calendar days before and after the sale.
Future recurring acquisitions enter the review only through explicit sourced
schedule records. Unknown spouse, outside-account and automatic reinvestment
coverage remain review issues. Substantially-identical replacements require human
assessment and explicit purchase permission; fees, overlap or correlation cannot
certify legal suitability.

Observed lot losses use complete matching inventory and exact remaining adjusted
basis. They are gross observations, with net tax effects unavailable where fees or
other evidence are missing. Review outputs never certify wash-sale safety or
promise tax savings, and never record trades. Humans review original broker facts
and execute every approved transaction themselves.
