# Bond research

Use your browser to verify current issuer information, bond terms, credit risks, maturity, call provisions, and pricing context. Use invest for existing bond valuation and explicit scenario calculations. Disclose valuation dates, yield assumptions, currency, and missing security-specific prices or increments. Do not generate automatic individual-bond trade instructions without verified inputs. Compare relevant funds where useful using existing exposure tools. Prepare a report for human review and manual execution; never authenticate with a broker or submit orders.
