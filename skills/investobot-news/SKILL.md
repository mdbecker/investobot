# News, catalysts and thesis changes

Investigate material company developments through your authorized browser and
research tools. Investobot preserves immutable evidence and compares observations;
it does not browse, run this skill, score sentiment or make trading decisions.

Read [research-method.md](references/research-method.md) for the workflow,
[event-identity.md](references/event-identity.md) for stable identities,
[json-contract.md](references/json-contract.md) for authoritative input discovery,
and [change-review.md](references/change-review.md) for conservative deltas.

Load the effective historical context before researching. Recheck inherited
records; their last observation is not fresh verification. Reuse earlier stable
IDs when a development reappears. Missing observations never establish closure,
and disputed claims are leads requiring verification, not confirmed facts or
trading signals. Prefer primary sources, distinguish
verified facts from allegations and your interpretations, preserve uncertainty,
and document actual coverage. Never invent missing dates, sources or resolution.
Validate the JSON and preview its import. Obtain explicit human approval before
confirmation with `--yes`. Research never changes holdings, plans or accepted
investment decisions.
