# Accounting and comparability

- Exact financial values are decimal strings, never binary float calculations.
- Duration metrics retain both period boundaries. Instant balance-sheet values
  retain their as-of date. A balance is not a duration observation.
- Keep standalone quarter, year-to-date, annual and trailing periods separate.
  Nine-month cash flow cannot be labeled third-quarter cash flow without a
  separately supported quarter derivation permitted by the contract.
- Preserve fiscal calendars and reporting durations; differing periods cannot
  silently produce growth. Compare only matched definitions, bases, units,
  reporting entities, consolidated/segment scopes and comparable periods.
- Never sum or compare currencies without an explicit supported FX model.
- GAAP and non-GAAP remain separate. Record reconciliation limitations and
  company-defined metric definitions rather than treating adjustments as GAAP.
- Guidance describes future expectations, not disclosed historical results.
  Keep its source, target period, range and qualitative qualifications.
- Findings distinguish source-backed facts from agent interpretations. No score,
  ranking, consensus estimate or research-generated trade is required.
- Amendments/restatements preserve original and revised filing references and
  known differences. Research retrieval dates are distinct from filing dates and
  covered periods; never imply revised facts were known before publication.

Comparison uses saved evidence and Decimal arithmetic. An incompatible or missing
metric produces a warning and no calculated growth; a zero denominator cannot
produce a percentage. Accepted history is immutable.
