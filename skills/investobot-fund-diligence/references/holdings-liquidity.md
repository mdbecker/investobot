# Dated holdings and product-specific liquidity

Use one original snapshot and no more than 100 rows. Preserve the disclosed
weights and identifiers; never renormalize partial lists or invent security links.
FULL requires affirmative source evidence of completeness and every reported row.
TOP_N requires the source's actual top-list, in descending weight order (ties
allowed). PARTIAL states omissions. UNAVAILABLE has no date, source or rows.
Each row must cite the snapshot source or expressly identify the same snapshot.
Weights must be nonnegative and at most 100%; a long-only total above 100.5%
is unsupported rather than a signal to rescale.

Label totals as observed. A largest-ten sum needs at least ten observed positions;
it describes full portfolio top-ten concentration only for FULL or verified TOP_N
containing the actual ten largest names. Do not invent the unseen residual of a
partial list, sector allocations, issuer aggregation, derivative exposure or
underlying holdings of other funds. Accepted diligence is separate from provider
`fund holdings/compare/replacements` and household exposure.

ETF evidence may include published median bid-ask spread, signed premium/discount
and average daily dollar volume, each with its reporting window. Nonnegative
spreads and volume remain separate from prospectus expense ratios. Trading volume
does not establish reliable execution liquidity.

For mutual funds, record stated NAV dealing frequency, redemption restrictions,
fees and minimums in sourced LIQUIDITY findings. Do not assign ETF spread metrics
to mutual funds or shareholder NAV dealing assumptions to ETFs. No common
liquidity score or NAV arbitrage model is supported.

[SEC ETF bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-24)
provides background; extract measurements and mechanics from the fund's actual
dated disclosures rather than treating general educational text as fund evidence.
